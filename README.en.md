<h1 align="center">Today</h1>

<p align="center">
  Your tasks, always within reach.
</p>

A minimalist to-do list for Mac and Windows. Capture tasks, choose what matters,
and check things off — in a compact window that opens with one click on the app icon.

## Download

- **[For Mac](https://github.com/IwanBelenko/TodayBar/releases/download/v1.6.1/Today-1.6.1-mac.zip)** — macOS 13+, Intel and Apple Silicon.
- **[For Windows](https://github.com/IwanBelenko/TodayBar/releases/download/v1.6.1/Today-1.6.1-windows-x64.zip)** — Windows 11, Intel and AMD.
- **[For Windows ARM](https://github.com/IwanBelenko/TodayBar/releases/download/v1.6.1/Today-1.6.1-windows-arm64.zip)** — Snapdragon and other ARM devices.

## Everything you need for everyday tasks

- **Work and personal.** Use the built-in categories or create your own.
- **Important tasks first.** Set priority with flames: more flames move a task higher.
- **Stay on top of deadlines.** Set a date and time, get reminders, and spot overdue tasks.
- **Keep track of what you've done.** Find tasks with search and revisit completed ones in history.

Light and dark themes. Russian or English, chosen automatically from your system settings.

## Get started

**Mac:** unzip the download, move Today to Applications, and open it.

**Windows:** unzip the download and open Today.exe.

Your system may show an unverified-developer warning on first launch.
[Help with installation and security warnings](docs/GUIDE.en.md#install).

Today works offline with no account required. Your data stays on your computer;
there is no sync between devices.
